How it works

From the first sketch to the files you keep. Scope, deliverables, and timing agreed with you along the way.

  1. 01

    Step 1: Share your idea

    Send sketches, dimensions, photographs, or references — whatever you already have. A rough idea is a valid starting point, and there is no need to prepare drawings first.

  2. 02

    Step 2: Discuss the scope

    We talk through what the model needs to cover, what you want to receive at the end, and anything still undecided. Scope, deliverables, and timing are agreed here rather than assumed.

  3. 03

    Step 3: Develop and review

    The CAD model is developed and shared with you for review. Seeing the geometry is usually when the useful questions surface, and the model is adjusted as the design is discussed.

  4. 04

    Step 4: Agree and hand over

    Once the model reflects the design you want, we confirm the final deliverables agreed for the project and hand them over.

What we do — and what we don't

Clear boundaries keep expectations honest from the start.

Our scope

We develop detailed 3D CAD models and assembly layouts from your requirements — sketches, dimensions, references, or a description of what you need. The deliverable is a model that communicates the design clearly enough to review it, refine it, and hand it on for the next stage.

Outside our scope

CADSmitX provides CAD modeling and design communication. We do not carry out fabrication, installation, certified structural calculations, or electrical and control programming.
