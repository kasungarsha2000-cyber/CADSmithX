Motion Studies for Pick-and-Place Mechanisms

Animation, Basic Motion and Motion Analysis — which one answers your question

Table of Contents

  • Setting a study up so the results mean something

Working on something similar?

Exploring a pick-and-place mechanism? Share the pick and place positions, available space, and intended cycle.

Key takeaways

  • Choose the motion-study type for the question being asked.
  • Trace the gripper path and check the extremes of travel.
  • Treat motion results as design guidance, with assumptions stated.

SOLIDWORKS offers three levels of motion study, and picking the wrong one either wastes an afternoon or gives an answer that looks authoritative and is not. The choice depends entirely on what you are trying to find out.

Study typeWhat it modelsAnswers
AnimationPositions over time, driven by mates and keyframesWhat does the cycle look like; will it present well
Basic MotionMass, gravity, springs and contactDoes it fall, swing or collide as expected
Motion AnalysisFull kinematics and dynamics, with forces and plotsTorque, velocity, and actuator sizing inputs

Motion Analysis comes from the SOLIDWORKS Motion add-in, which must be switched on and is not in every licence level. Worth confirming before promising force plots to a client.

Setting a study up so the results mean something

  1. Fix everything that is genuinely fixed; the frame should not be free to drift.
  2. Make the moving sub-assemblies flexible, or they will travel as one rigid lump.
  3. Add motors where the real drive acts — a rotary motor on the drive shaft, a linear one on the cylinder rod.
  4. Give the motor a realistic profile: constant speed is rarely what a servo does; use distance or a data-point profile where it matters.
  5. Run, then use Trace Path on the gripper to see the real envelope rather than the intended one.
A motion study is not a safety case

Results depend on the masses, friction and contact settings entered. Treat them as design guidance for sizing and clearance conversations, and have loads, guarding and safety functions verified by the responsible engineer against the applicable standards.

The most valuable output is often the least dramatic: an interference check run across the whole cycle, at the extremes of travel. A mechanism that clears everything at mid-stroke and catches a guard bracket at full extension is a problem worth finding on screen.
